Read CLAUDE.md first.

Device review of the redesign. One real design problem, one bug, and some polish.

============================================================
1. TIER CHANGES ARE INVISIBLE — the important one
============================================================

I switched Hard to Medium and today's task list did not change. I assumed it was
broken. It is not — the day-start snapshot means the change lands tomorrow, which is
correct and deliberate.

The failure is that nothing said so.

Fix: whenever a pending change exists — tier, tasks, or targets — the app says it
plainly, without me hunting for it.

- **My Challenge** shows a persistent line at the top: `MEDIUM STARTS TOMORROW — today
  is still HARD, 6 tasks.` Include the new task count so the consequence is concrete.
- **Home** shows a quiet one-line note under the task list when a change is pending.
  Not a banner, not dismissible — just a fact, in textMid.
- The **tier badge in the header** shows today's actual tier, never the pending one.
  Today's truth belongs in the header; tomorrow's belongs in the note.
- Pending changes must be **cancellable**, and cancelling says so.

Derive all of this from the pending-change state that already exists. Do not add a
second source of truth.

============================================================
2. THE TIERS SHOULD DIFFER IN SUBSTANCE, NOT JUST COUNT
============================================================

Right now the tiers differ by how many tasks they carry. They should differ the way
the real variants of this challenge differ — in what the tasks actually demand.

Set these as the tier defaults. Treat them as defaults a user can then edit in My
Challenge, not as immutable rules.

**HARD — 6 tasks.** The traditional 75 Hard.
  Workout 1 — 45 min
  Workout 2 — 45 min, outdoors
  Gallon of water
  Read 10 pages — non-fiction
  Follow the diet — no cheat meals, no alcohol
  Progress photo

**MEDIUM — 5 tasks.** Serious, sustainable.
  Workout — 45 min
  Move again — 30 min, any form
  3 litres of water
  Read 10 pages
  Follow the diet — one planned cheat meal a week

**SOFT — 4 tasks.** Real, but survivable alongside a life.
  Workout — 45 min, one rest day a week
  3 litres of water
  Read 10 pages
  Eat well — alcohol only on social occasions

Requirements:

- These live in the tier config as data, with the descriptor text alongside each task.
  The descriptor is what makes a tier feel different — "no cheat meals, no alcohol"
  versus "one planned cheat meal a week" is the entire distinction between Hard and
  Medium, and it must be visible on the task row and the check-in card.
- Editing any target still relabels to CUSTOM exactly as it does today. Do not touch
  `displayTierLabel()`.
- Existing challenges keep the task set frozen in their snapshots. This changes what a
  **new** day inherits, never what a past day recorded. Confirm that explicitly — an
  active challenge must not silently gain or lose a task tonight.
- The tier picker cards show the real task list for each tier, so the choice is
  informed rather than a number.

Report what an existing Hard challenge sees tomorrow after this lands. If the answer is
"the tasks change underneath them," stop and tell me before doing it.

============================================================
3. BUG — a timer runs for a task that is already complete
============================================================

Home shows Workout 1 struck through, completed 3:11 AM, while the mini-bar counts down
"Workout 1 — 45 min · 25:06".

Completing a task by any route — checkbox, swipe, or Health suggestion — must cancel
its running timer, dismiss its notifications, and clear the mini-bar. Check the reverse
too: what happens if a timer finishes for a task already completed by hand.

============================================================
4. THE COMPLETED CHECK-IN SCREEN IS MOSTLY EMPTY
============================================================

With all tasks done, the card fills most of the screen to hold three lines. It is the
emptiest moment in an app whose whole job is to make finishing feel earned.

Either the card shrinks to its content, or the space earns itself. My preference,
strongest first:

  - Today's completed tasks as a satisfying list — what you actually did
  - The Wall, so a finished day immediately shows the day it filled
  - Tomorrow's task set

Pick one, keep it calm, and do not invent a new component if The Wall already exists.

============================================================
5. SMALL THINGS
============================================================

- `0-DAY SQUAD STREAK` reads like a bug. On zero, say `SQUAD STARTS TODAY`. Same for a
  personal streak of 0.
- `WHY I STARTED` renders as bare quote marks when empty. Either setup is not saving
  the why — check that first, it may be a real bug — or the empty state needs copy and
  a way to add one.
- Home's squad strip is very sparse for the space it occupies. Tighten it, or let it
  carry each member's progress the way the Squad tab does.

CONSTRAINTS
============================================================

No changes to the snapshot model, completion logic, RLS, or service contracts. Expo
stays on 54. Bundle ID unchanged. `npx tsc --noEmit` and `npx expo lint` clean.
