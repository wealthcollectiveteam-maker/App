PHASE 10C — Make the UI tell the truth

Read CLAUDE.md first. All standing constraints still apply.

Governing rule for this whole phase:

  THE UI MUST NEVER CLAIM SOMETHING HAPPENED THAT DIDN'T.

A control that does nothing, a toast that reports a success that never
occurred, a counter that describes a step that doesn't exist, a toggle that
forgets — each of these is a lie to the user. I am about to hand this link to
five friends. Every lie costs me credibility with them personally.

=============================================================

Walk all 19 routes in a real browser. For EVERY interactive control — button,

  Route | Control | What it appears to do | What it actually does | Verdict

PART 2 — Fixes I have already decided
=============================================================

2.1 Progress photo (HARD tier)
  Capture and storage were never built. It becomes an honest checkbox.
  - It stays a real task and counts toward the day exactly like the others.
  - Swipe completes it. No camera, no upload, no storage bucket.
  - The card copy must say plainly that the photo stays on your phone — the app
    does not store or upload it. Something like: "Take it on your phone. Ranked
    never sees it."
  - Remove any dead camera/upload/storage code paths rather than leaving them
    disabled. Report anything you delete.

2.2 Nutrition — manual entry, USDA removed
  - Delete the USDA FoodData Central lookup entirely. It 404s on ordinary meals
    like "chicken and rice" and it is not going to get good.
  - Replace with manual entry: meal name plus calories, protein, carbs, fat.
    All optional except the name.
  - Meals must still scope to the current day only. There was a bug once where
    meals accumulated across the whole challenge — verify by querying, not by
    looking at the screen.
  - If a USDA API key exists in .env or config, remove it and tell me. Confirm
    by grep that it is not in the emitted bundle.
  - Nutrition is not a scored task in any tier. Do not make it one.

2.3 The share button on finish.tsx
  It toasts "Results copied to share" and copies nothing, on every platform.
  Either make it genuinely copy, or remove the button. Your call — but the
  toast must not appear unless the copy actually succeeded. If you make it work,
  prove it by reading the clipboard back in the browser.

2.4 "Step 2 of 3" with no step 3
  Find it, work out what the real count is, and make the label match reality.

2.5 Notification preferences
  Currently in-memory only — every toggle resets on launch. Persist them.
  On web, notifications do not exist at all: if the toggles cannot do anything
  on this platform, say so in the UI rather than offering a control that
  silently does nothing. Do not show a switch that cannot switch anything.
