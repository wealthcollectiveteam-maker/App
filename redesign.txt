============================================================
PART 0 — DECISIONS. Read before anything else.
============================================================

You raised four things after reading new-ui.pdf. All four are correct. Here are the
rulings — they override the mockups where they conflict.

**1. The third tier stays SOFT. Do not add a CUSTOM tier.**

You are right that this is a domain change wearing a design change's clothes. CUSTOM is
already the label `displayTierLabel()` derives at render time when a target sits below
its tier standard — that meaning was chosen deliberately and is live. A selectable
CUSTOM tier would give one word two meanings, and would touch TIERS, the tier column,
and day-start snapshots to do it.

The mockup is overridden. Render the third card as **SOFT**, keep all three penalty
behaviours exactly as built, and leave `displayTierLabel()` alone.

**2. "Miss a task, restart at day one" is Hard-only. Move it.**

`restartsChallenge` is true for hard and false for the other two, so as a blanket line
under the heading it is simply false for two thirds of users. Move the consequence
inside each tier card, worded for that tier:

  HARD    — Miss a task, restart at day one.
  MEDIUM  — Miss a task, the streak resets. Your day count continues.
  SOFT    — Miss a task, the streak resets.

Derive the wording from the tier config, not from three hardcoded strings, so it can
never drift from the rules again.

**3. Type: two families do the work. A third is allowed for the wordmark only.**

Inter (400/500/600/700/900) for all UI, plus one serif italic for quotes and
descriptors. If a condensed italic display face meaningfully improves the RANKED
wordmark, you may add it — but **only** for the wordmark and the large day numeral,
where it appears once per screen and can be subset small. If that turns fiddly, Inter
900 italic with tight tracking is an acceptable stand-in. Do not introduce a third face
for body, labels, or buttons.

**4. The splash colour must move with the ground, and this one is urgent.**

Correct. ship-prep set splash and adaptive-icon background to `#151724`, and neither can
ship over the air. Update both to the new ground colour as part of Part 1, in the same
commit as the tokens — not later. A cold launch that flashes navy into a near-black
first frame would be permanent until the next binary.

Report the exact values you set and where, so I can confirm before the production
build.

============================================================
PART 2 — Build the primitives once
============================================================

Every screen assembles from these. Build them properly and Part 3 is mechanical.

- **Skew** — the parallelogram. skewX '-12deg' on the container, counter-skew '12deg'
  on the inner label so the shape slants and the text stays upright. Filled variant
  (tier badge, primary actions) and outlined variant (TIMER, START TIMER, SAVE ENTRY,
  PING). **Get the counter-skew right** — slanted text inside a slanted box reads as a
  rendering bug, not a style.

- **SegmentBar** — N discrete segments with gaps; filled = accent, empty = surfaceAlt.
  Under the day number on Home and on every squad member row. Segment count is always
  that member's own task count. Never hardcode 6, and never use the viewer's count for
  someone else's row — that bug already bit us once.

- **TheWall** — 75 cells, 15 across x 5 down. States: done (accent fill), today
  (outlined), future (surfaceAlt), and a distinct marker on cell 75. Legend below:
  DONE · TODAY · DAY 75.

- **DiamondBadge** — 45deg-rotated square with an upright numeral inside. Locked =
  textLow outline; earned = accent.

- **StatBox** — large figure over a tiny uppercase label.

- **TaskRow** — square checkbox, label, right-aligned meta. Completed rows strike
  through and drop to textMid.

Primary buttons are now **solid accent with dark text** — GO TO CHECK-IN, START DAY 01.
This reverses the old outlined-only rule. Secondary actions stay outlined.
