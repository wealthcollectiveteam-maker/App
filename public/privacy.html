Ranked Fitness

Privacy Policy

Last updated 24 August 2026

Ranked Fitness is a challenge-tracking app you run with a small group of friends. This policy explains exactly what the app collects, what it never touches, and what the people in your squad can and cannot see about you.

The short version

Two things never leave your phone

Apple Health data. If you grant Health access, the app reads it into memory to show you your numbers. It is never transmitted, never written to our database, and never logged.

Progress photos. The app has no camera access and no photo storage. When a challenge asks for a progress photo, you take it with your own camera app and keep it yourself. Ranked Fitness never sees it.

What the app stores

When you use Ranked Fitness, the following is saved to our database so it is there when you come back:

  • Your email address — used only to send you a one-time sign-in code. There is no password.
  • Your display name and profile details you choose to enter.
  • Your challenge — the tier and length you picked, your task list including any you added yourself, and which tasks you completed on which day.
  • Your streak and history — completed days, missed days, and milestones.
  • Anything you log — journal entries, meals and their macros, body metrics such as weight, and workout notes.
  • Squad membership — which squads you belong to, invite codes, activity items and pings.

What your squad can see

This is the part people most want to know, so it is stated plainly.

Your squadmates can see: your display name, which day you are on, how long your challenge is, your tier, how many of today's tasks you have completed, and your streak.

Your squadmates cannot see: your journal entries, your meals or macros, your body metrics, or your workout logs. Not a summary of them, not a count, nothing.

This is not enforced by hiding a screen. It is enforced in the database itself with row-level security, so those records are unreadable to anyone but you even if someone bypassed the app entirely and queried the API directly.

What we don't do

  • We do not sell your data. To anyone. Ever.
  • We do not show advertising in the app.
  • We do not use third-party advertising or analytics tracking.
  • We do not build a profile about you for any purpose other than running your challenge.

Where it is stored

Data is stored with Supabase, a hosted PostgreSQL provider, and is protected by row-level security policies as described above. Sign-in codes are delivered by email.

Deleting your account

You can delete your account from inside the app at any time: Settings → Delete account. This removes your account and the data associated with it — your challenge, your history, your journal entries, your logs, and your squad memberships.

Deletion is permanent and cannot be undone. If you would rather have it done for you, email the address below from the address on your account.

Children

Ranked Fitness is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect information from them. If you believe a child has created an account, contact us and we will remove it.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes in a way that affects what is collected or who can see it, the date at the top of this page changes and the updated policy is posted here.
